What you can track

Mood
Rate how you feel, how stressed you are and how long you slept. It takes about a minute.
Journal
Write freely, add tags, and search everything you've written.
Symptoms
Note what happened, how severe it was and what might have triggered it.
Medication
Log each dose as taken, missed or skipped, and see how consistent you are over time.
Therapy
Keep appointments and notes together, with reminders and a calendar view.
Habits
Check in once a day and watch your streaks and completion rate build.

How it works

Check in

Log what you want, when you want. Nothing is required every day.

Review

Charts and a calendar show how sleep, stress and mood move together.

Share

Export a report as PDF, Excel or CSV for your therapist or doctor.

Your records are yours

Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.
Each entry belongs to your account and isn't shown to other users.
You can export your data whenever you like.
You can edit or delete any entry at any time.
